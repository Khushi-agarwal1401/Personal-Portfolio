About Me

I am Khushi Agarwal, a Computer Science student currently pursuing a B.Tech in Computer Science at PW Institute of Innovation. I completed my 11th and 12th grade with Computer Science, where my interest in technology and problem-solving began to grow. I enjoy learning new concepts, solving challenging problems, and continuously improving my technical skills.

I have foundational knowledge in HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Basic Python with NumPy, MySQL, and Object-Oriented Programming (OOP). I am actively working on strengthening these skills and expanding my understanding of software development and modern technologies. My goal is to keep learning, building projects, and gaining practical experience that helps me grow as a developer.

Beyond programming, I have a long-term vision of contributing to India’s technology ecosystem. My dream is to build India’s own mobile and computer brand in the future, which I plan to name “Indus”. The name is inspired by the Indus Valley Civilization, symbolizing strength, innovation, and India’s rich history.

Through Indus, I want to support the “Make in India” movement by creating affordable, high-quality devices designed specifically for Indian users. My vision is to help reduce dependence on foreign technology products while building a strong, proudly Indian brand. I also hope to create job opportunities, encourage innovation, and inspire others to dream big and build something meaningful for the future.
